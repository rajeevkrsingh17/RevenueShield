# RevenueShield — Buildathon Demo Guide (4–5 Minutes)

Follow this step-by-step walkthrough script for judging demonstrations:

## ⏱️ Step-by-Step Demo Script

### Step 1: Landing Page Overview (30s)
1. Open application at `http://localhost:3000/`.
2. Highlight Tagline: *"Protect Revenue. Recover Payments. Act Intelligently."*
3. Show the **Autonomous Pipeline Flow** (Detect &rarr; Diagnose &rarr; Predict &rarr; Simulate &rarr; Recover).
4. Click **[Explore Demo]** or **[Launch Dashboard]**.

### Step 2: Executive Overview Dashboard (45s)
1. Point to the **5 KPI Cards**:
   - Total Processed: **₹52.4L**
   - Successful Revenue: **₹43.8L**
   - Revenue at Risk: **₹7.8L**
   - AI Recoverable: **₹5.1L**
   - Revenue Recovered: **₹2.7L**
2. Note the animated number counters, percentage changes, tooltips, and SVG sparklines.
3. Show the top Critical Leakage Banner (**Bank X Failure Spike**).

### Step 3: Module 1 — Revenue Intelligence & Root Cause Analysis (60s)
1. Click **Revenue Intelligence** in the sidebar.
2. View the **Payment Processing Revenue Funnel** (Attempted &rarr; Authorized &rarr; Captured &rarr; Settled &rarr; Recovered).
3. Click **[Investigate]** on the **Bank X Failure Spike** card.
4. Walk through the **Root Cause Investigation Panel**:
   - What happened: 31% failure spike in UPI.
   - Why: Bank X switch degradation.
   - Evidence & stats.
   - Click **[Simulate Strategy Options]**.

### Step 4: Key Differentiator — Recovery Strategy Simulator (60s)
1. In the Simulator, select the **₹4,999** transaction (`tx_4999_key`).
2. Show the **Strategy Comparison Matrix**:
   - Do nothing (0% prob, ₹0 EV)
   - Retry immediately (61% prob, ₹3,048 EV)
   - **Retry after 90 sec** (86% prob, **₹4,299 EV**, Low friction)
3. Highlight AI Rationale quote & safety policy validation.
4. Click **[Simulate Strategy]** / **[Approve Action]** & trigger confetti!

### Step 5: AI Copilot & Natural Language Querying (45s)
1. Open **AI Copilot** (floating button or sidebar).
2. Click preset query: *"Where are we losing the most money?"*
3. Show evidence card citing **1,842 Bank X UPI transactions** and metrics.

### Step 6: Immutable Audit Log (30s)
1. Open **Audit Log**.
2. Verify the newly approved recovery action is recorded with timestamp, policy status, and outcome.
